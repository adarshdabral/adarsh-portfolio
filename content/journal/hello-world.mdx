Every developer starts a blog at least three times in their life, and finishes maybe one post per attempt. This is attempt number... let's not count.

Here's the deal I'm making with myself: short is fine. A paragraph about something I fixed, learned, or got annoyed by counts. Nobody needs another 2,000-word "10 Tips" post.

## What this actually is

A place to write down the small stuff that doesn't fit anywhere else — a bug that turned out to be interesting, a tool that saved an afternoon, a half-formed opinion about how software should be built.

If you're reading this, you found the Journal app. Poke around the rest of the desktop too.
